Built to help you verify information.

VeriTruth combines AI analysis, source checks, and transparent explanations to help users assess the credibility of online stories.

Our mission

VeriTruth helps researchers, journalists, and everyday users review information with clear evidence, transparent scoring, and understandable reporting.

Three-stage verification pipeline

1

Input

Users submit article text, URLs, or images for analysis.

2

Signal extraction

The platform evaluates source trust, language pattern, bias cues, and similarity.

3

Report

VeriTruth generates a verdict and explanation to make the outcome easier to interpret.

Signal components

AI-based detection

Applies trained classification logic to evaluate content patterns and confidence.

Multiple input methods

Supports text, URL, and OCR-based image analysis in one platform.

Source analysis

Reviews the source and reference signals that may affect overall trust.

Transparent explanations

Provides human-readable reasoning that makes the decision process easier to follow.
