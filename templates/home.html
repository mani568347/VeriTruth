Understanding your result

What Your Result Means

VeriTruth shows different signals to help you understand the analysis. Each score has a different meaning.

Prediction

FAKE / REAL Example

This is the BERT model's classification of the submitted content. It is the model's best judgement, not a guaranteed fact.

Model Confidence

83% Example

This shows how confident the BERT model is in its selected prediction. Confidence refers to the model's certainty about the class it chose — it is not a guaranteed probability that the article is true or false.

Trust Index

49% Example

This is a combined score based on the available supporting signals, such as:

  • Source authority
  • Similarity
  • Headline alignment
  • Language & bias
  • Recency
  • Author info

These measures should be read together. No single score should be treated as absolute proof.
